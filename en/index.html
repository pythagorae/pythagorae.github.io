Software, AI, high-performance computing and space-systems engineering.

We build software that turns raw, complex data into something you can compute on.

What we build

  • Earth-observation data processors Ground-segment processing chains for satellite missions — synthetic aperture radar (SAR) focusing, including spotlight modes, and optical / ocean-color processors, from raw downlink to calibrated products.
  • Sovereign AI inference Compute platforms for LLM inference and token generation that run on local infrastructure, built in stages from pilot to production.
  • AI in orbit On-board AI computing for spacecraft: processing data where it is acquired, before it reaches the ground.
  • Optimal decision solvers Search, planning and game-theoretic solvers for decisions under uncertainty, exposed as tools that AI agents can call.
  • Engineering & consulting Research and development, high-performance and distributed systems, technical assistance and training — for industrial, scientific, space and government clients.
